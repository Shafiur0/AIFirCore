**Speaker 1 (Team Leader):**

### [1:30 – 2:45] PART 2 & 3: SOLUTION & END-TO-END WORKFLOW
**Speaker 2 (Member 02):**
> "Our solution is **AI Fitness Brain (AIFitCore)**—an autonomous, real-time personal fitness and nutrition companion accessible straight from any browser.
> 
> Here is how the end-to-end user journey works:
> 
> First, **Intelligent Onboarding**: The user inputs their biometrics, lifestyle, workout experience, available equipment (whether gym weights or bodyweight), and any past injuries. Our system uses verified clinical Mifflin-St Jeor formulas to calculate their exact Basal Metabolic Rate and Daily Caloric targets.
> 
> Second, **Personalized Workout Generation**: The user chooses from Gym Training, Home Workouts, or Calisthenics. The engine creates a tailored routine calibrated to their exact physical ability.
> 
> Third, **AI Camera Trainer**: When a workout begins, the user positions themselves in front of their phone or laptop camera. Our AI watches their movement in real-time, displays their skeletal pose, counts repetitions automatically, checks joint angles, and provides live voice feedback like *'Go lower on your squat'* or *'Keep your spine straight'*.
> 
> Fourth, **Nutrition & Recovery Reconciliation**: Users track meals through natural language or our localized South Asian food database, while a daily recovery audit adjusts future workout intensity based on sleep and muscle soreness."

### [2:45 – 4:00] PART 4: AI APPROACH & TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE
**Speaker 3 (Member 03):**
> "To deliver this securely and efficiently, our technical architecture is built on three core pillars:
> 
> 1. **Client-Side Edge Computer Vision:** We use **Google MediaPipe Pose Landmarker** to track 33 3D skeletal landmarks at 30+ frames per second. All geometric angle calculations happen 100% locally in the user's browser using WebAssembly. This means **zero video is ever sent to the cloud**, guaranteeing complete healthcare privacy and zero expensive GPU server costs.
> 
> 2. **Deterministic Metabolic Engine:** Instead of letting LLMs guess numbers, all BMR, TDEE, and safety thresholds are calculated deterministically in TypeScript, protecting underage or underweight athletes from unhealthy deficits.
> 
> 3. **Context-Grounded Conversational AI:** We integrate OpenRouter with structured JSON reasoning. The AI assistant receives real-time database context—such as remaining calories, protein deficits, and fatigue scores—delivering grounded, hyper-personalized coaching without hallucinations.
> 
> 4. **Production Engineering:** We haven't just designed a slide deck—we have already engineered, tested, and deployed a working production application on Next.js 15, Supabase PostgreSQL with 25 secured tables, and live hosting on Vercel at `aifitcore.vercel.app`."
